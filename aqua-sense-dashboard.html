About these readings

“No configured alerts” only means the readings passed this prototype’s pH, temperature, and LDR checks. It does not mean the water is drinkable or safe for any use. These sensors do not test for germs, metals, pesticides, or many other hazards. LDR output is raw and light-related; it is not a turbidity measurement. Have water tested by an appropriate laboratory and follow local health guidance before drinking it.
